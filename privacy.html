Privacy policy for Ai-shorts

Last updated: 2026-10-29

Ai-shorts is a personal tool by the owner of the Neuralyze YouTube channel. It uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own channel.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission (Google sign-in), the tool can upload videos to the owner's channel and read the status and statistics of the owner's own videos (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API).

What it stores

An OAuth token for the owner's account, kept privately by the owner. Nothing else about the owner is stored. The tool does not collect data about viewers or any other person.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. Ai-shorts' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Control and deletion

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete stored data, email neuralyze.yt@gmail.com; we delete it within 30 days.

YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
